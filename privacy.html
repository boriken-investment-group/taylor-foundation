Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

What we collect

When you use the request form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, your street or neighborhood, your foundation type, how soon you need help, and what you describe in the form. That's all. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

What we do with it

We share your request with one local foundation repair company that serves Taylor, so they can contact you about your home. We don't sell your details, and we don't share them with anyone else or add you to a mailing list.

How the form works

The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a third-party service that receives your submission and forwards it to us by email. Their privacy policy applies to that step, and they keep a copy for a limited period.

Deleting your details

Email derek@borikeninvestmentgroup.com and we'll delete your request. Once it has been passed to a repair company, you'll also need to ask that company directly.

Who runs this site

Foundation Repair Taylor TX is an independent referral service operated by Boriken Investment Group, Round Rock, Texas. We are not a foundation repair contractor.
