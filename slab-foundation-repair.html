Slab foundation repair in Taylor, TX

Most Texas homes built in recent decades sit on a concrete slab poured directly on the ground. In Taylor that ground is blackland clay, which swells when it's wet and shrinks when it's dry. A slab can't flex much, so when the soil under one part of it moves more than another, the stress shows up as cracks in the slab and in the house above it.

Signs on a slab home

  • Doors and windows that start sticking or won't latch
  • Diagonal cracks above door frames and at window corners
  • Stair-step cracks in exterior brick
  • Cracks in tile floors or visible cracks in the slab edge outside
  • Gaps opening between walls and ceilings, or baseboards pulling away

One hairline crack is common and often cosmetic. Change is what matters, so photograph cracks with the date and check them again after a season.

How slab repair generally works

The usual approach is to support the slab on piers that reach deeper, more stable ground, then lift the low areas back toward level. Common pier types include pressed concrete piers, steel piers and drilled concrete piers. Which one suits your home depends on your soil and on the engineer's assessment, not on what a particular company happens to install.

Moisture is part of the picture too. On expansive clay, keeping the moisture around the foundation more consistent through drainage, gutters and grading is often discussed alongside the structural fix. Ask how the plan you're given deals with water as well as with piers.

What Taylor requires

A slab foundation repair in Taylor needs a city permit, and the city's checklist requires a stamped and signed engineer's plan of the whole foundation identifying the area to be repaired. See the Taylor permit guide for the full list.

Questions to ask before you sign

  • Who is the engineer, and do they work for me or for you?
  • How many piers, where, and why that number?
  • Will you pull the Taylor permit, and can I have a copy?
  • What does the warranty cover, and does it transfer if I sell?
  • How does your plan deal with drainage and moisture?
